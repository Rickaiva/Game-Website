About

Ruinik is a 2d pixel game created using pygame,
an immersive gameplay and a variation of combo styles
in a combat. Explore the ruined grounds and learn more
about the mysteries presented ahead of you.

Game Features

Immersive Gameplay

Engage players through storytelling, visuals,
realistic audio, and interactivity. It allows players
to feel a strong connection to the game, enhancing their overall experience.

Different Combo Styles

Offers different combo styles for the players to
fully enhance their gameplay, such as the
ability to switch to a variation of weapons for the players.

Different Enemy Variations

Providing players a variation of enemy
types, which challenge players and allows
them to develop strategies to defeat each enemy type.
