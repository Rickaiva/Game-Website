About

Ruinik is a 2d pixel game created using pygame,
an immersive gameplay and a variation of combo styles
in a combat. Explore the ruined grounds and learn more
about the mysteries presented ahead of you.

Characters

Jag (Main Character)

A merchant man who’s a normal person despite living in a magical world. But his ordinary life will turn upside down after a not so ordinary adventure.

Rolvan

A mage who hides a lot of mystery.

Game Features

Immersive Gameplay

Engage players through storytelling, visuals, realistic audio, and interactivity. It allows players to feel a strong connection to the game, enhancing their overall experience.

Different Combo Styles

Offers different combo styles for the players to fully enhance their gameplay, such as the ability to switch to a variation of weapons for the players.

Different Enemy Variations

Providing players a variation of enemy types, which challenge players and allows them to develop strategies to defeat each enemy type.
